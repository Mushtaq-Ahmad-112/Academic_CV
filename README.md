# Academic CV Template | Scholarship Applications

Welcome to the **Academic CV Template** repository!

This repository contains an editable Microsoft Word academic CV template designed for students, researchers, and aspiring scholars who want to prepare a professional CV for fully funded scholarships, PhD positions, research opportunities, and international academic applications.

This is the same CV format I developed while preparing my own academic applications. Using my application materials and academic profile, I have secured **more than 10 scholarships and fully funded opportunities across the world**. I am sharing this template to help other students create and improve their academic CVs.

## 📄 What's Included?

* **Editable Microsoft Word Template:** Download the CV format and customize it according to your academic background, research experience, and achievements.
* **Academic CV Structure:** Includes sections covering personal information, professional summary, education, research experience and projects, research interests, publications, conferences, webinars, technical skills, employment experience, languages, and references.
* **Beginner-Friendly Guidance:** Learn how to organize your information and present your academic profile clearly and professionally.
* **Regular Updates:** This is an evolving template that I will update over time to improve its structure, presentation, and usefulness.

## 🚀 How to Use This Template

1. **Download the template:** Open the Word document available in this repository and download it to your computer.
2. **Watch the tutorial:** Follow the complete YouTube video to understand the purpose and structure of each section.
3. **Customize your CV:** Replace the sample information with your own academic qualifications, research experience, publications, skills, and achievements.
4. **Keep it accurate:** Include only genuine qualifications, experience, publications, awards, and other relevant information.
5. **Tailor it to your application:** Adjust your CV according to the requirements of the scholarship, university, research group, or PhD position.
6. **Review before submitting:** Check the formatting, grammar, consistency, and accuracy of all information before sending your application.

## ⚠️ Important Note

This template is a starting point, not a guarantee of scholarship selection. A successful application depends on several factors, including academic qualifications, research fit, relevant experience, application quality, and the specific selection criteria.

Do not copy another person's achievements or include information that does not apply to you. Use this template to present your own profile honestly and effectively.
